## 0.1.0

Initial public release of source-neutral selection, redacted planning, ownership-aware
reconciliation, Doppler and caller-owned Infisical readers, exact Convex deployment
and project/type defaults operations, the shared CLI, and optional Alchemy integration.
Requires Node.js 22.18+ and ESM. Live production delivery is not part of the release's
verified consumer scope.
